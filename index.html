FAQ

Questions, answered.

Will the protection change how my marble or wood looks?

No. The film is crystal clear and designed to keep the natural color, veins and grain. You can choose a glossy or matte finish to match your surface.

Which surfaces can you protect?

Natural marble and stone countertops, tables and vanities, natural wood furniture, and glass windows and facades for homes, offices, showrooms and commercial spaces.

How long does installation take?

Most home projects are completed in a single visit. Our team will confirm the timing after inspecting your surfaces.

Is there a warranty?

Yes. Prolong installations come with a 5-year warranty.

Can I pay in installments?

Yes. Installments are available through Souhoola and valU.

How much does it cost?

Pricing depends on the surface type and area. Send us your measurements or photos on WhatsApp for a quick quote, or book a free visit.
